Explore Notezy Features

List & Grid View

Notezy allows users to switch between list and grid view modes, providing a flexible interface that adapts to your workflow. Whether you prefer a linear overview or a visual mosaic layout, you can quickly toggle between views to better manage your notes, projects, and reminders. This feature ensures that your notes are not just stored but presented in a way that makes sense to you, reducing visual clutter and improving focus.

Create & Edit Notes

With Notezy's seamless note creation and editing tools, users can jot down thoughts, plans, or inspirations anytime. The interface is optimized for both quick entries and detailed documentation. Rich text editing, bullet lists, and intuitive formatting tools make it ideal for students, professionals, and creatives alike. Your notes auto-save in real time, so you never lose progress—even if you close the app unexpectedly.

Delete Notes with Confirmation

To prevent accidental loss of important information, Notezy incorporates a confirmation step before deletion. This extra layer of verification ensures that users have time to reconsider before removing a note. The app also offers a temporary recycle bin, where deleted notes can be restored within a grace period. Peace of mind is just one of the many user-centered safeguards we’ve built in.

Attach Images

Images speak louder than words, and with Notezy, adding them to your notes is effortless. Whether you're inserting diagrams, screenshots, or inspirational photos, image attachments enhance your notes visually. Each image is displayed inline and stored securely, supporting drag-and-drop functionality and gallery previews for a richer user experience.

Pin Important Notes

No more hunting for key information. The pin feature ensures your most essential notes stay at the top of your list. Whether it’s a shopping list, project deadline, or inspirational quote, pinned notes remain visible and prioritized until unpinned, helping you focus on what truly matters each day.

Live Search

Notezy’s live search function makes finding specific notes incredibly fast. As you type, results are filtered in real time across both titles and body content. This dynamic searching experience helps you retrieve information instantly, making your workflow smoother and more efficient, even with hundreds of notes.

Label Management

Organize your notes effortlessly with customizable labels. Create, rename, and delete labels to sort notes by categories like work, personal, ideas, or deadlines. Label colors and filters allow for fast visual scanning, giving you complete control over how your digital workspace is arranged.

Filter by Labels

Quickly narrow down your notes by filtering based on one or more labels. Whether you’re looking for all notes tagged 'Meeting' or 'Personal Goals', Notezy’s filtering system helps you focus your view, eliminating noise and highlighting what’s relevant at the moment.

Password-Protected Notes

Security is paramount. Notezy allows you to protect sensitive notes with passwords. Each protected note requires authentication to open, adding a vital layer of privacy to your most confidential information. This is perfect for storing personal thoughts, private work documents, or any data you want to keep strictly to yourself.
